KYC and Wallet Activation Explainer

You need KYC to send transactions on Redbelly mainnet. You do not need it to hold RBNT on an exchange or wrapped RBNT on another chain.

Last verified 18 Oct 2026

Native vs wrapped

Native mainnet activity means sending transactions from a wallet on Redbelly Network. That needs KYC. Wrapped or bridged RBNT lives on another chain such as Ethereum or Base. Redbelly's access check does not apply there [S3] [S8].

DAO vs network regions

The DAO is open to every region for general members [S9]. Redbelly Network does not offer services in 18 jurisdictions listed in clause 15.1 of its terms [S5]. These are two separate rules.

Part B. The five common questions

1. Do I need KYC, and when?

Yes to act on Redbelly mainnet. No to hold RBNT elsewhere.

Redbelly gives write access only to accounts that hold an access credential from an accredited issuer [S3] [S4]. That covers sending, swapping, bridging and staking on mainnet. RBNT on an exchange, and wrapped RBNT on other chains, is outside that check [S7] [S8]. An exchange can still ask for its own KYC.

2. How many wallets can one verified identity cover?

Up to 10 wallets.

After your first account is enabled, Redbelly Access lets you add more accounts to the same identity [S2]. When 10 are linked, the portal hides the Add another account button and shows the notice "Account allocation exhausted" [S2]. No official page describes going past 10. Use one of your 10 wallets, or ask Averer in a ticket (Part D). The portal also warns that you answer for everything a linked wallet does on the network.

3. How long does approval take?

Usually a few minutes. Rarely, a few days.

That is the portal's own message while your check is pending [S2]. Press Check verification status to refresh. If it is still pending after a few days, open an Averer ticket (Part D).

4. Can I join from my country?

The DAO: yes, from any region. The network: not from 18 listed jurisdictions.

Redbelly DAO

A DAO vote that closed on 5 July 2026 removed the regional restriction for general DAO members [S9] [S10]. Geographic limits still apply to High Council members and DAO leadership roles [S9].

Redbelly Network

Clause 15.1 of the terms lists where Redbelly does not offer services. Clause 15.2 says tax residents of, or people located in, those places may not use its website, platform, software or tools [S5].

The 18 jurisdictions: Afghanistan, Central African Republic, North Korea, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Guinea-Bissau (spelled Guinea-Bussau in the terms), Iran, Iraq, Lebanon, Libya, Myanmar, Russia, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, Syria, Ukraine, Yemen and Zimbabwe [S5]. The DAO vote changed the DAO's own membership rule. It did not change the network's terms.

5. Is KYC needed to stake, and why?

Yes. Staking happens on Redbelly mainnet.

Staking is done by calling a deposit function on the staking contract, which is a mainnet transaction [S6]. Only enabled accounts can send transactions [S3]. Staking through an exchange or another chain follows that venue's rules, which this guide does not cover.

Part C. Get verified and activate a wallet

Redbelly Access is the official portal. Averer is the accredited issuer that checks your identity [S2] [S4]. Button names below are the ones the portal shows [S2].

Before you start

  • MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet, the wallets the portal names [S2].
  • A valid passport. Verification uses a photo ID plus a biometric check [S3]. Averer handles your documents. The portal says that data is not shared with or stored on Redbelly Network [S2].
  • The wallet address you want to use. It becomes your public address on Redbelly Network [S2].
  • No fee for you. The issuer covers the fees to grant access [S2].
  • Check the 18 jurisdictions in Question 4 first.

Steps

  1. Connect. Open access.redbelly.network. Type the address yourself. Press Connect wallet and approve in your wallet. If the network is missing, press ADD NETWORK MANUALLY and use the table below [S2] [S14].
  2. Start. On the Get access credential screen, press Begin identity verification.
  3. Continue to Averer. Read the notice, tick I understand, then press Continue with Averer.
  4. Sign. Your wallet asks you to let the issuer access your public address. Sign it.
  5. Verify. A window titled "Interacting with averer.co" opens. Submit your passport and finish the biometric check [S3].
  6. Wait. The portal shows Identity verification pending. Press Check verification status to refresh.
  7. Enable. When approved, your account shows Account status: Not enabled. Press Enable account and sign in your wallet. The issuer sends the RBNT that pays for this. If the portal says you have not received the RBNT yet, wait a few minutes and press RETRY.
  8. Done. The account shows Enabled and the portal shows Next steps. The wallet can now send transactions, bridge and stake on mainnet.

Add another wallet to the same identity

You do not verify again. Open the portal with a wallet that is already enabled and go to Accounts [S2].

Same wallet app, other address

Press Add another account. In your wallet, switch this site's connection to the address you want to add. Sign the request to link it to your verified identity.

A different wallet app

Press Link another wallet. Use the wallet button in the top right corner of the portal to connect it, then sign.

Then press Enable account for the new wallet. If the portal says the account must first be funded, send the stated amount of RBNT to it from an enabled wallet and try again. If it says the address "is attached to another account", it already belongs to another identity and cannot be added.

Part D. When something goes wrong

Identity problems go to Averer. Connection or account errors go to Redbelly Support [S2].

Approval is taking too long.

Most checks finish in a few minutes, and the portal says rare cases take a few days. Refresh with Check verification status. If it is still pending after a few days, open an Averer ticket below [S2].

The wallet limit is reached.

You cannot add an 11th wallet to one identity. Use one of the 10 you have. No official page describes raising the limit, so ask Averer in a ticket if you need to [S2].

The wallet will not connect or the account will not enable.

Add the network by hand with the table in Part C. Then use Redbelly Support, which points to the Redbelly Discord [S15].

How to open a ticket on the Averer portal

  1. Open the Averer help centre. It is the Contact support destination from averer.co [S11] [S13].
  2. Press Submit a ticket, or go straight to the ticket form [S12].
  3. Fill in your full name, email address and a subject. Under What is your issue?, say what the screen shows, for example "Identity verification failed". Enter the wallet address you used in Redbelly Access in the Redbelly Wallet Address field, which is required. You can attach files.
  4. Tick I'm not a robot, then press Submit. Never put a seed phrase or private key in a ticket. The form only needs your wallet address.

Check the portal yourself

The portal is an app, so its text sits in its public script files. Open access.redbelly.network on a computer, press F12, open Sources, press Ctrl+Shift+F and search for these exact phrases: Account allocation exhausted, could take a few days, Begin identity verification, Link another wallet, Do I need to pay for access. File names change with each release, so the search finds whichever version is live.
